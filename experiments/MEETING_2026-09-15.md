## Fair comparison prepared

`tools/compare_team_agents.py` runs the Q agent and DQN score r1250 separately
against three rule-based agents, training off, with the same scenario and seed.
It preserves every per-round row and reports:

- mean score and 95% confidence interval;
- margin against the mean opponent and each individual opponent;
- the assignment's gate-4 decision;
- strict-first, tied-first and behind-top frequencies;
- suicide, kill-round, bomb and invalid-action rates.

The comparison does not overwrite either live model. A seed now initializes the
world plus Python and NumPy RNGs, so both processes begin from the same random
streams. Their trajectories can still diverge after their actions change the
game state.

Run from the repository root:

```bash
./venv/bin/python tools/compare_team_agents.py --rounds 200 --seed 211
```

N=200 is the first valid comparison. If either result is near zero or becomes
the intended submission candidate, confirm it at N=1000 on a new seed before
making the claim in the report.
